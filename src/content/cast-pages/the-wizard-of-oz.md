intro: >-
  Casting is incredibly difficult, and this show was probably the most difficult
  we’ve had to cast in as long as I can remember. We spent hours checking,
  double checking and making sure that everyone in this show will have a lot of
  stage time. Please do not look at the movie as a guide to what you will be
  doing in our show – we’re really excited to pay honor to the film, but forge
  our own path as well!


  Everyone is in A LOT in this show. Most actors are listed on the cast list in 5 or more places. To help you keep track of the groups you are in, there is a tab in this workbook that lists each actor and the groups they will be called in on the rehearsal schedule.


  Actors may be called by GROUP or by CHARACTER NAME. All actors are part of a group and have specific roles in that group.


  For every actor you need to know 1) the groups they are in and 2) the characters they will be playing.


  For example, PROFESSOR MARVEL may be called on the schedule as part of the CARNIVAL FOLK group, but also may be called by name as PROFESSOR MARVEL at the end, as the other CARNIVAL FOLK aren’t in the final scene.
