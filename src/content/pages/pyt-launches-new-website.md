We've spent some time polishing up our website. Take a look around and let us know what you think! The urls and pages you know (and love?) should all still be there, but clearer, and there are some new ones, including:

A [Shows](/shows/) page - your one-stop-shop listing all PYT's current productions. [Center Stage Musicals](/musicals/), [Stories on Stage](/stories-on-stage/), and [Studio Shows](/studio-productions/).

[A fancy new Calendar ](/calendar/)(um, just a Google Calendar?) with all our dates added so you can see what's when.

A home for our beloved [Stories on Stage Youth Company (SOS)](<>), listing all the shows coming up, and how to audition for the next season.

An [Auditions page](/audition/), that lists all productions currently open for auditions, some tips and tricks for audition day, and good old FAQs. AND, a [Casting page](/casting/) that talks about PYT's approach, the whys and wherefores, what comes into the decisions, and PYT's philosophy on the whole thing.

[School Tickets](<>) - a new place for school to find which productions are currently offering group bookings and school tickets, and how to grab them.

Of course we couldn't leave out ways to [Support PYT](/ways-to-support/), especially as there are *so many cool new ones.* Please take a look 💖.

Lastly for now, WATCH THIS SPACE for exciting new things coming soon, including....a shop 😎.
