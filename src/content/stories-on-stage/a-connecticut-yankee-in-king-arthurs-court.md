  A modern kid on a castle tour gets accidentally transported to the court of King Arthur, and her cell phone definitely does not have service. Based on Mark Twain’s classic, this fun retelling is full of laughs as our hero navigates courtly manners and medieval sensibilities to find her way back home.


  Based on the book by Mark Twain.
